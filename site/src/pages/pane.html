How these were made. The first image is a screen capture of the Claude app. The rest are drawn by the pane's own code from a real session's state — the session that built this website — exported at 2×. No mockups, no invented numbers.

Home

One thing to do next.

  • Next up — the highest-priority item, with a Use button that puts the right prompt in your box.
  • At a glance — how full the context is against your mark.
  • Tasks — Claude's task list, kept after the turn ends: Working, Waiting, Paused or Done.
  • Agents, Now, Last decision — the short version of every other page.
  • The footer says which version runs and whether it's current.
All tasks

The plan, out loud.

Every step of the session's task list with its state. Yes — that's the actual plan for building this page, caught at step 2. The uncle documents everything.

Running

Who's on the clock.

Live agents, background commands and scheduled wake-ups, each with its side, model, elapsed time and what it's doing now — and a Stop button where one can be stopped. Captured on a quiet moment: nobody's spending.

Guide

What now, by priority.

Open FAILs first, then unasked questions, owner decisions, things to escalate, background agents, memo and context hygiene, budget. Advice only — settled by Claude after a re-check with the evidence traced, never on a bare claim.

Resume

Where was I?

Now, Next, what's Left and what Needs you, from the project's work memo — plus the last receipts with their check and open lines. After a break or a /compact this is all you need. The top receipt is the real 2.13.0 release.

Decisions

Who decided what.

By you — every question you answered. By Judge — every question the repo could answer without bothering you, with its evidence. Nothing gets asked twice.

Agents

The bill, itemised.

What was saved against your main model, the spend by model, and one row per agent: side, model, effort, tokens, dollars, verdict. This session: 24% saved — $3.27 instead of $4.30. Opus did the heavy work; the reading cost two cents.

Overrides

House rules.

Everything you changed from the defaults, for this project and for all projects. Here: Git guard on deny, five agents at once, Opus · Medium for the main session. Every key.

Editor

Click, don't type.

Every simple key as a field or a menu, grouped, with a one-line hint. Choose This project or All projects, then Save — it writes the ## abofles overrides block for you. Reset puts a key back.
